# SmartCess Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27. This policy covers the SmartCess Android MVP.

## What stays on your phone

Your bills, reminders, documents, emails, appointments, routines, and
conversation turns are stored in an on-device database. Camera framing
guidance (move closer, a little left, ready) runs entirely on your phone
and never sends video anywhere.

## What we send, and only when you ask

- **Bill or document capture:** the single photo you capture is sent to the
  Meta Model API (Muse Spark) for extraction, only after you tap capture or
  say "capture".
- **Voice commands:** microphone audio streams to Muse Voice Transcribe only
  while the Talk screen (or "say capture") is listening.
- Nothing is sent in the background, and nothing is sent without your action.

## What we never do

SmartCess never pays bills, sends email, submits forms, navigates, or makes
calls. It never gives medical, legal, or financial advice. Committing actions
(save, schedule, calendar, archive, delete) always ask you first.

## Analytics and crash reports

Both are **off by default**. If you opt in under Settings, SmartCess records
metadata only (which screen, whether an action succeeded) — never images,
transcripts, or personal details. Crash reports contain only technical logs.

## Your controls

- Export your data as JSON or CSV from Settings to a file you choose.
- Delete everything from Settings at any time (confirmation required).
- Remove the API key from Settings to force fully offline mode.
- Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use Send feedback in Settings.
