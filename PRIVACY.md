# SmartCess Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04. This policy covers the SmartCess Android app.

## What stays on your phone

Your bills, reminders, documents, emails, appointments, routines, and
conversation turns are stored in an on-device database. Camera framing
guidance (move closer, a little left, ready) runs entirely on your phone
and never sends video anywhere.

## What we send, and only when you ask

- **Bill or document capture:** the single photo you capture is sent to our
  backend, which forwards it to the Meta Model API (Muse Spark) for
  extraction — only after you tap capture or say "capture".
- **Voice commands:** your recording uploads to our backend for transcription
  (Muse Voice Transcribe) only while Talk is recording, and is deleted after
  processing. Talk's own speech is never treated as a new command.
- Sign-in uses Google; the backend holds only signed session tokens
  (15-minute access, 7-day refresh). Nothing is sent in the background, and
  nothing is sent without your action.

## What we never do

SmartCess never pays bills, sends email, submits forms, navigates, or makes
calls. It never gives medical, legal, or financial advice. Committing actions
(save, schedule, calendar, archive, delete) always ask you first.

## Analytics

Analytics are **off by default**. If you opt in under Settings, SmartCess
records metadata only (which screen, whether an action succeeded) — never
images, audio, transcripts, message text, keys, or personal details. There is
no crash reporter.

## Your controls

- Export your data as JSON or CSV from Settings to a file you choose.
- Delete everything from Settings at any time (confirmation required).
- Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use Send feedback in Settings.
