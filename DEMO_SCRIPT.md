# SmartCess — 3-Minute Demo Video Script

Total: ~180 seconds. Record with TalkBack ON so viewers hear the spoken UX.
Captions on. One continuous phone screen recording + brief intro/outro cards.

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook (20s)

[Card: "SmartCess — life admin you can hear."]
Narrator: "For blind and low-vision users, a paper bill is a locked door. SmartCess
turns inaccessible life admin into independent action — voice-first, TalkBack-first,
and safe by design. Watch."

## 0:20–0:55 — Daily Briefing (35s)

[Home → tap Listen on the Today card.]
Narrator: "The day starts with a spoken briefing — overdue bills, appointments,
urgent email, routines due. One card, three most urgent items, everything else one
tap away. Fully usable by voice or touch."

[TalkBack reads the briefing; show deep-link rows.]

## 0:55–1:45 — Guided bill scan (50s)

[Home → Scan Bill. Hold phone over a real bill.]
Narrator: "Now the core moment: guided scanning. The app talks you into position —
'I do not see a document,' 'Move closer,' 'Hold straight' — with haptic ticks, all
computed on-device so it works offline."

[Capture on "Ready. Tap capture or say capture." Say "capture" aloud.]
Narrator: "One photo goes to the AI. It extracts the provider, amount, and due date
— then asks me to review before anything is saved. Low-confidence results are
flagged, never silently filed."

[Show Bill Result screen: editable fields, low-confidence banner, spoken summary.]

## 1:45–2:20 — Compare + remind (35s)

[Tap Compare, then Set reminder; confirm the dialog.]
Narrator: "SmartCess compares against last month's bill and explains the change. One
tap sets a reminder — but notice: every save, reminder, and calendar write asks for
confirmation first. It never pays, emails, or calls on its own."

[Show comparison with percent change; reminder confirmation dialog.]

## 2:20–2:45 — Offline + safety (25s)

[Toggle airplane mode; rescan or open Email.]
Narrator: "No signal? Framing and reading keep working on-device, clearly labelled
'Offline mode. Please review.' And private data stays on the phone — analytics are
opt-in, keys are encrypted, and everything can be exported or deleted in Settings."

## 2:45–3:00 — Close (15s)

[Card: GitHub URL + "Free core. Built with Hillarry."]
Narrator: "SmartCess: transcribe listens, Spark thinks, ML Kit guides — and you stay
in control. Code, README, and roadmap are on GitHub. Thank you."

---

### Recording checklist

- [ ] TalkBack enabled, speech rate normal, media volume up
- [ ] Real (or realistic sample) bill; good lighting; backup bill ready
- [ ] Demo inbox seeded (fresh install seeds 3 emails)
- [ ] Airplane-mode segment rehearsed; offline banner visible
- [ ] Captions added; no real API keys, account numbers, or personal data visible
- [ ] Final cut ≤ 3:00; export 1080p; upload unlisted + link in README/README release
